Rare Avian Sightings including the rare migratory Smew made at Assam's Laokhowa Wildlife Sanctuary in Nagaon, Kaziranga Tiger Reserve

During the recent 7th Kaziranga Waterbird Count 2026 conducted across Rowmari, Dondowa and Muamari beels in the Laokhowa buffer of the Kaziranga Tiger Reserve, the rare migratory Smew (Mergellus albellus) was recorded for the first time at the sanctuary as well as sightings of the critically endangered Bengal florican were made. The Smew breeds in Northern Eurasia and migrates south in the winters. These bird sightings indicate a healthy ecosystem that has the capability to harbour rare species and offer them resources to thrive amidst global stressors.

The forest department is strengthening biodiversity monitoring further and also planning to take strict action on offenders breaching illegal boundaries, which has already led to an arrest for illegal fishing in the sanctuary.
