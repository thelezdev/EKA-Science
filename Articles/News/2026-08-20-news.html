Fastest Known Star Could Reveal the Spin of the Milky Way's Central Black Hole

Researchers at the Max Planck Institute of Extraterrestrial Physics (MEP) discover the closest known star to the Milky Way Galaxy's Supermassive Black Hole, Sagittarius A*. Dubbed the S301, it is the fastest known star travelling at greater than 8% the speed of light and its highly eccentric relativistic orbit holds the potential to reveal the spin of Sagittarius A* through longitudinal research. It may also shed light on the formation and/or cause of the proximity of other S stars to the Supermassive Black Hole.

Sleep-like Brain Activity Restores Memory Consolidation in Awake, Sleep-Deprived Mice

Researchers at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, including Giulio Tononi, induced the characteristic ON/OFF cortical activity of slow-wave sleep in awake mice using optogenetics. The induced patterns resulted in reduced the need for local sleep and restored memory consolidation after sleep deprivation, suggesting some functions of sleep may be dependent on specific patterns of brain activity than sleep as a whole

IIT Guwahati Researchers Develop A Pathway for Efficient CO₂ Capture and Microalgal Biofuel Production

Researchers at IIT Guwahati find an improved CO₂ capture method using microalgal culture, also giving better efficiency in collecting residual biomass and subsequent conversion to standard biofuel. The implications of the paper to potentially be implemented in industrial settings will be interesting to follow against real engineering and economic constraints
