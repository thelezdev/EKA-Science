Google's Deepmind predicts all possible DNA mutations for the entire human genome

Google's Deepmind AI has predicted the change to every single nucleotide letter change in the human genome and created the AlphaGenome Atlas. The AI computed predictions for each of the 3 possible nucleotide changes across every DNA letter in the human genome. With over 1 pentabyte worth of data, all available data is available for non-commercial use and academic research. Reserchers came up with a value called the AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) score, which helps in interpreting which mutations are genuinely worth paying attention to, which need longer observations and point to holes in our knowledge. A fascinating case for AI helping previously untenable research.
