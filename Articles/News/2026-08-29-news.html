Researchers urge to push the development of antivenoms for regional species

In a collaborative paper including researchers from Tezpur University, Institute of Advanced Study of Science and Technology and Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, it was found that the antivenom used for Trimeresurus popeiorum were largely inadequate to treat bites from Trimeresurus erythrurus, the red tailed bamboo pit viper, found extensively in North East India. This addresses a broader issue in current antivenom treatments which prescribe the antivenom of the big 4 snakes to treat regional differences, often at the cost of inadequate treatment for people.
