About

EKA Science is an independent science organization founded in North East India. We aim to highlight scientific efforts and research in regions broadly overlooked by established scientific media and seek to develop as well as conduct scientific research in the near future.

We recognize the necessity of good scientific literacy amongst the general populace, especially in the hyper polarized climate of the modern times. We, therefore, focus on essential science communication through reviews of academic papers to simplify complex work for the non-expert and the student alike, delve into more technical and pedagogical work for veterans and experts in their fields, write engaging pieces to evoke the fancy of curious individuals. To put the spotlight on the hard work of the experts and scholars, we seek to engage in in-depth interviews to better understand their perspectives, publications and the broader trends happening in respective fields. Our goal is to strengthen scientific communities and encourage more collaborations between researchers, institutions and fields; we thus create a platform to ease the navigation process through greater outreach, for the public and researchers.

We also seek to build research infrastructure that can aid researchers at all levels in their analyses and data collection and possibly conduct our own research with continued growth.

Science is a method; not a thing nor a dogma. The scientific method is one of humanities greatest achievements and a testament to the power of collective effort. While papers can be gated behind journals and paywalls, science remains one of humanities greatest and longest running open project where anyone can contribute. Curiosity, rigor, effort and some good luck, and maybe YOU can have a good finding or theory that just so slightly adds to the collective knowledge of humanity.

Editorial Standards

To maintain independence with rigor, drafts of academic paper reviews are sent to the authors for any concerns of misrepresentations but not permission for publication. We hold the final verdict on whether a piece or an article gets published. We also consult experts whenever necessary to maintain the standards of good science communication.

Privacy Policy

EKA Science believes in the ethos of open-source technology and a free internet. We also value privacy. We seek to collect minimal data only when necessary. All data collection shall be explicit and transparent. User data is never sold to any third-parties or institutions. All data voluntarily collected can be taken down, if practical. We do not track users across sites.
